What is Kayamata and is it safe? +
Kayamata is a Northern Nigerian tradition of love and attraction herbs, often presented as organic body and beauty products that enhance intimacy and connection. All our Kayamata products are carefully formulated from natural ingredients and are safe for use as directed.
What is an Egbe Appeasement and who needs it? +
Egbe is a Yoruba spiritual concept — a heavenly peer group. People with unresolved Egbe issues often experience repeated misfortune, relationship failures, stagnation, or strange afflictions. Appeasement restores harmony with your Egbe and unlocks your true destiny. Contact us for a consultation to find out if this applies to you.
